DACQ: 4-bit LLM Quantization
Investigated whether modeling transformer weight distributions improves LLM quantization. Showed that weights in Llama3-8B and Qwen2.5-7B are best fit by a logistic distribution (109/192 and 111/168 submodules), then built DACQ, a post-training quantization framework that applies per-layer CDF companding before 4-bit quantization. DACQ reconstructs weights more accurately than AWQ, but perplexity and MMLU do not improve: outlier weights are rare but carry model knowledge, and distribution matching under-represents them.
Lower reconstruction error than AWQ · Co-authored arXiv paper · Feb 2026

Scaling BERT4Rec Training Efficiency
Scaled the BERT4Rec sequential recommender from 85M to 430M parameters on Amazon Electronics and profiled training across NVIDIA A100, V100 and L4 GPUs. Combining mixed precision (AMP) with torch.compile cut step time on the 130M model from 278 ms to 58 ms and peak memory from 11.2 GB to 7.2 GB. Also implemented instance-wise LoRA (iLoRA), training under 1% of parameters at 2.85 GB peak memory.

Edit-REPA: Representation-Aligned Diffusion for Image Editing
Team project applying representation alignment (REPA) to instruction-based image editing, where editors often change parts of the scene they should leave alone. I fine-tuned InstructPix2Pix (Stable Diffusion v1.5) for 80K steps with an added loss that aligns U-Net mid-block features to a frozen CLIP vision encoder. Training stayed stable, alignment plateaued at ~0.8 cosine similarity, and side-by-side edits preserved scene structure better than the unaligned baseline.
Qualitative proof of concept · ~0.8 feature alignment · 80K fine-tuning steps
